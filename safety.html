Safety Tips

Effective August 1, 2026 · version safety-v1

The short version

Meeting new people is the whole point of ENSO — doing it safely is yours. These tips don’t cover everything, and no app feature replaces your own judgment. In an emergency, call 911.

Before You Go

  • Tell a trusted friend or family member where you’re going, when, and who you’re meeting. Share your live location with them if you can.
  • Look at the event details and the host’s profile. Verified badge, trust score, and how long they’ve been on ENSO are all signals.
  • Have your own plan for getting there and getting home that doesn’t depend on anyone you’re meeting for the first time.

Meeting Up

  • Meet in public places, especially the first time. Daylight and busy venues are your friends.
  • Consider bringing a friend — ENSO events are better in groups anyway.
  • Keep your phone charged and with you.
  • You never owe anyone your address, your workplace, or a ride.

Alcohol & Substances

  • Know your limits and stay in control — it’s much harder to make good safety decisions when impaired.
  • Watch your drink being made and never leave it unattended.
  • Never feel pressured to drink or take anything. Anyone who pressures you is telling you something important about themselves.

Trust Your Gut

  • If something feels off, it probably is. You can leave any event or conversation at any moment — you don’t owe anyone an explanation.
  • It’s okay to be "rude" to stay safe. A ride home costs less than ignoring your instincts.
  • Hosts: if an attendee is making others uncomfortable, ask them to leave, and report it in the app.

Reporting, Blocking & Emergencies

  • Use Block to immediately cut contact with anyone. They won’t be told you blocked them.
  • Use Report (⋯) on profiles, messages, posts, and events to alert our safety team. Reports are confidential.
  • If a crime occurs or anyone is in danger, call 911 first — then report it to us so we can act on the account.
  • The app is never a substitute for emergency services.
